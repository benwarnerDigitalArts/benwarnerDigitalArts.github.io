Ben Warner: Games!

PhageShift:

The brain child of a team of three: Hayden, Derek, and me (Ben).

This game features randomly generated obstacles, collectibles, and enemies. The enemies have some AI path finding and seek to destroy the Phage.

Summary: A Bacteriophage, for some reason, decides to fight Coronavirus-like enemies. Shoot to kill and get in the bloodstream to progress! After each stage, the field grows!

SpiderCrane - on the Web:

I created this game with the goal of implementing some crane behaviors, but added some of my own variations on the standard crane concept.

Instead, I thought it would be amusing to make a spider with the front legs behaving as cranes. This was more complicated than necessary, yet amusing regardless.

Summary: Pick up the flies that jiggle around randomly and eat them to win! Crawl around, switch modes, or switch front legs which act as the "cranes".

aMAZEing:

I created this game with the goal of demonstrating basic controls and spawning objects in a maze-like experience.

Of course, I tried to put an interesting spin on it... I am egg-static at the results!

Summary: An egg rolls through a "fridge" to gather ingredients. The fridge opens after collecting enough items for an omelette, but what happens next!?
