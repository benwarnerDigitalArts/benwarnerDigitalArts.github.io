Product Designs:

Here are brief glimpses into the products that I have designed which consists of weldments, sheet metal, sensitive equipment, and electrical enclosures.

Retrofits:

These retrofits were designed with the intention of being 3D printed to enable rabid prototyping and immediate implementation.

This material was feasible due to being in low-wear and low-stress environments in some cases while also enabling break-away functionality in some cases.

My earlier works are related to optimizing world-class automation equipment at Boeing. More can be read about those systems here:

  • Electroimpact FlexTrack Systems
  • Electroimpact Whitepaper on Quadbot Systems

Fixture Assemblies:

These fixtures were designed to hold parts of varying sizes in place while inspecting with automated measuring tools and considering GD&T callouts, like CMMs.

I use ergonomic designs that fully constrain degrees of freedom while aslo considering manufacturability and ease of assembly as verified by a team of machinists.

My fixture design work at was primarily focused on holding Stryker joint replacement components in place on CMMs (Coordinate Measurement Machines). More can be read about those components here:

  • Stryker Orthopaedics Joint Replacement components

Personal Projects:

In my spare time, I design and 3D print some assemblies for simple functions as needed.

I have investigated a variety of 3D modeling software and found that AutoDesk Fusion 360 or OnShape have the most inclusive toolset for my projects.

SolidWorks for Makers is also suitable for my needs and relates well to my background of extensive professional work using other tiers of SolidWorks.

While designing and printing these relatively simple projects, I also investigate and retrofit my 3D printer as needed to improve quality and repeatability.
