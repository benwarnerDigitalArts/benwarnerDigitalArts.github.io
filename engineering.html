Product Designs

Here are brief glimpses into the products that I have designed which consists of weldments, sheet metal, sensitive equipment, and electrical enclosures.

These assemblies were developed with skills in reverse engineering, flow analysis, and structural simulation. They feature complex piping and electrical routing, following strict ASME compliance.





Retrofit Parts:

These retrofits were designed with the intention of being 3D printed to enable rabid prototyping and immediate implementation.

Using 3D printed material was feasible for these applications due to being in low-wear and low-stress environments in some cases.

In other cases, being 3D printed allowed for break-away funtionality when other components are more critical.

Fixture Assemblies:

These fixtures were designed with the intention of holding parts of varying sizes in place while measuring with automated measuring tools and considering GD and T callouts, like CMMs.

I focus on making ergonomic designs that fully constrain degrees of freedom, while aslo considering manufacturability and ease of assembly.

I work closely with a team of machinists to ensure that my design meets their expectations.

Personal Projects:

My Thingiverse Page with some of my project files

In my spare time, I design and 3D print some assemblies for simple functions as needed.

I have investigated a variety of 3D modeling softwares and found that AutoDesk Fusion 360 or OnShape have the most inclusive toolset for my projects.

SolidWorks for Makers is also suitable for my needs and relates well to my background of extensive professional work using other tiers of SolidWorks.

While designing and printing these relatively simple projects, I also investigate and retrofit my 3D printer as needed to improve quality and repeatability.
